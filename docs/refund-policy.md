## Refund Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

### Try before you pay

Every WorkCrew account includes a free trial. No payment details are required to
start it. We provide the trial so you can evaluate WorkCrew fully before you
spend anything.

### Subscriptions

Because a free trial is provided before any payment is taken, **subscription
payments are non-refundable**, except as set out below.

### When we do refund

We will always refund:

- **Duplicate charges**, where you were billed more than once for the same period.
- **Accidental or unauthorised charges**, including a renewal taken after you had
  already cancelled.
- **Any refund we are required to provide by law.** If you are a consumer in the
  European Union or the United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to withdraw
  from a purchase of digital content within 14 days, subject to the conditions
  set out at checkout. Nothing in this policy limits that right.

### Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Cancelling stops all
future billing immediately. You keep full access until the end of the period you
have already paid for. We do not charge a cancellation fee.

### If something is wrong

If WorkCrew is not working as described, contact us before requesting a
chargeback. We would rather fix the problem or make it right. Most issues are
resolved the same day.

Contact: support@getworkcrew.com

## Notes for the owner (do not publish this section)

- Set up `support@getworkcrew.com`, or change the address above to one that is
  monitored. Providers check that the contact address works, and an unreachable
  support address is a rejection reason on its own.
- The EU and UK withdrawal right is not optional and cannot be written away. A
  Merchant of Record normally handles the consent wording at checkout for you.
- The "contact us before a chargeback" line is deliberate. Chargebacks cost the
  sale plus a fee, and a high chargeback rate terminates merchant accounts.
  Inviting contact first measurably reduces them.
